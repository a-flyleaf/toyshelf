--- layout: none intro: |- Roleplaying is one of the first ways I was social on the world wide web. For the unfamiliar: You take a character and write about them doing things, and other people reply and write about their own characters doing things with yours. Collaborative, improvisational storytelling---although kid!me didn't think of it that way. I just thought it was a fun way to kill time. Of all the short-lived, one-off characters I've spawned for RPs, [Slade Auctor](INCURL/misc/slade) has managed to be the most memorable. *Why,* I'm not exactly sure. He was made for [’Souls RPG](https://soulsrpg.com/forum/), a forum I was frankly a little too young for but which welcomed me with open arms anyway. At the suggestion of some newbie character creation guide (which I cannot find again for the life of me), I modeled Slade loosely after myself. His surname translates, simply, to "[author](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/auctor)." So, yes, he's a writer. No, he doesn't have opposable thumbs. On paper (pun not intended) he might've been turned into a thumb-having not!werewolf, with time and character development; in practice he never got there. Nowadays he sports a thick coat of amber, encased 5ever in my files and now this Toyshelf site. And yes, I designed this specific page as a #throwback to early-aughts web design. Colors and grungy textures and shadow effects and a photoshopped banner with song lyrics! Wow!! Did you know I cut my teeth on HTML/CSS through RP post templates? --- Slade Auctor (custom profile) | {{site.name}}
